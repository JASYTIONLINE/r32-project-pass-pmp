# Structured Exam Preparation Course - Home
Pass the PMP® on the First Try!

What if you could skip the noise and study exactly what PMI tests—no filler, no guesswork?  
That’s this guide. Every click here moves you one step closer to *a passing score and a better paycheck.*  

## 19 Steps on Your Path to Certification
Follow the Ten steps to Victory in this Structured Self Study Course to prepare for the Project Management Institute's Project Management Professional Certification Exam. This site contains the same material (and then some) you will receive in any 35 hour PMP Exam Prep Course. We have synthesized the knowledge contained in several PMP preparation Courses, boot camps, and in PMI's Exam Content Outline and Study guides. 

This site aggregate that information into a clean, clickable interactive experience you can finish on your schedule—page by page, lesson by lesson.  When you complete this section, you’ll have a complete understanding of the information you will need to pass the PMP Certification Exam on the first try. 
## The Promise  
Study this path from start to finish.  
Do the exercises.  
When you reach the last page and pass the practice quizzes, you will be ready to pass. Period. 
Or your money back.

## Section Contents
1. [Domains, Task, & Enablers (DTE): ](02-structured/01-dte/index.md) Understanding how Domains, Tasks & Enablers apply to the Core Concepts of Project Management (PM) is key to applying the facts you learn here to the logic you use to answer the questions on the PMP Exam. 
2. [The Lifecycle of a Project: ](02-structured/2-lifecycle/index.md)Understanding the order and flow of a Project will assist you in knowing what comes next which is what many Exam Questions ask.
3. [Business Environment: ](02-structured/3-business-environment/index.md)Start where projects really begin—why they exist, who they serve, and how they create value.  You’ll see the big picture before you ever touch a schedule.
4. [Start the Project: ](02-structured/4-stp/index.md)Stakeholders, teams, and kickoff. Learn how to launch like a pro and build momentum that lasts.  
5. [Plan the Project: ](02-structured/5-ptp/index.md)  Scope, schedule, risk, and budget—the plan that turns intent into control.  Templates, tips, and examples drawn straight from PMI’s playbook.  
6. [Lead the Project Team: ](02-structured/6-ltt/index.md)  Real leadership starts here. Communication, motivation, conflict resolution—everything that keeps a team producing under pressure.  
7.  [Support Performance: ](02-structured/7-sup-per/index.md)  Keep the engine running. Manage change, measure progress, and drive continuous improvement while staying calm in the chaos.  
8. [Close the Project: ](02-structured/8-ctp/index.md)  End strong. Capture lessons, confirm benefits, and hand off like a professional.  
9. [Prepare for the PMP Exam: ](02-structured/9-test-prep/index.md) Passing the PMP Exam is not just about knowing all the fact. Learn valuable insight in how to prepare for and sit the exam.
10. [Practice, Practice, Practice: ](02-structured/10-quiz/index.md) It is time to Fail Forward.  Don't wait until you think you can pass to start practicing the exam.  Each Question you get wrong provides valuable feedback.  Take a practice test today.
