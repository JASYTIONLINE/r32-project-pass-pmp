# 🧠 PMP Targeted Review Tool

## 👋 Welcome

This site is built for one purpose:

> To help you study smarter by focusing **only** on the PMP exam questions you've **missed** — and connecting them directly to the **PMI DTE (Domains, Tasks, and Enablers)** they relate to.

It doesn’t replace your learning. It’s a **launchpad** for your deeper research, reflection, and targeted practice.

---

## 🧩 How This Site Works

When you open the site, you'll see:

- ✅ A **brief welcome message**
- 📌 A button to start reviewing **your missed questions**
- 🧾 A navigation link to view this README

Each question displayed includes:

| Section | What It Does |
|--------|---------------|
| **Question** | The actual prompt from your test |
| **Options** | All the answer choices you were shown (A–D or more) |
| **Answer** | A full paragraph explaining the correct choice and why the others were wrong |
| **Exam Cue** | A small hint or “tell” that should have guided you to the right answer |
| **DTE Code** | The exact PMI ECO code (e.g., 2.3.1) tied to this question's concept |

---

## 🔎 How to Use This Site

> This site **does not tell you how to pass the PMP.** It helps you find where and why you struggled — and points you in the right direction.

1. **Review a question**
   - Read the prompt and all options
   - Read the explanation paragraph
   - Look at the **exam cue** — what should have tipped you off?

2. **Check the DTE**
   - Use the DTE code (e.g., `1.1.2`) to go back to your PMI resources:
     - ✅ PMI Study Hall  
     - ✅ PMBOK Guide  
     - ✅ Agile Practice Guide  
     - ✅ Any other trusted source

3. **Do your follow-up work**
   - Research the topic yourself
   - Study that concept more thoroughly
   - Rewrite your own explanation if needed

---

## 💬 What This Site Does *Not* Do

- ❌ It will not give you new PMP questions  
- ❌ It will not guess what’s on the exam  
- ❌ It will not walk you through the entire syllabus  
- ❌ It will not answer every question for you  

This is a tool for **reflective review** — not a one-stop shop.
