## Bonus Material
Just a little bit extra. The information contained in the [[02-structured/index.md|Structured Exam Prep Course]] is everything you need to pass the test, but if you are looking to gain a deeper understanding of Project Management (PM), or you need an alternate way to study, check out this bonus material.
## [[02-structured/9-test-prep/index|TEST PREP:]] 
1. [Tips and Tricks:](1-test-prep.md) Learn how to prepare for and take the Project Management Institute (PMI) Project Management Professional (PMP) Certification Exam
2. [Bloomberg's Taxonomy: ](05-bonus/2-test-prep/3--Understanding Terms.md)Discover the importance of synthesized knowledge that goes beyond memorization of facts.  Move to a new level of comprehension and internalize PMI core concepts which is critical to passing the PMP Exam. 
3. [12 Project Management Core Principles: ](3-principles.md) Your key project performance and behavior. These are not rigid rules, but universally applicable values that empower decision-making across project lifecycles and diverse industries.  
4. [[4-eco|PMI Exam Content Outline (ECO):]] While the *PMBOK® Guide*s explains what project management is, the ECO defines how it will be tested.
## [[02-structured/2-lifecycle/index|Project Lifecycle:]] 
Every project is a living breathing entity which follows a specific path from inception to end.  Learn about the Project Lifecyle and its associated Process Groups and Processes. 

## [Inputs. Tools, Techniques, and Outputs (ITTOs):](05-bonus/4-ittos/index.md)
Every Process and Process Group uses certain ITTOs to accomplish the work and feed the next process in the Project Lifecycle. Gain a better better understanding of how each ITTO fits within the 5 Process Groups.

## [EEFs & OPAs](5-eef-opa.md) 
External Environmental Factors and Organizational Process Assets  are similar but have very different effects on a Project Manger's decision making process.  Lear the difference and how they influence your organization.
